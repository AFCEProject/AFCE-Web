From actions to Effects

An action specifies a command. An Effect connects that command to the robot’s response and the resulting scene change—while remaining decodable into continuous actions.

Method

Two stages connect future-grounded representation learning to closed-loop control. Shared Effect tokens accommodate single-arm and bimanual interfaces.

1

Construct the target

Fuse demonstrated actions, realized robot responses, and sparse visual transitions into temporally ordered Effect tokens.

2

Predict the Effect

Train a flow-matching VLA to predict the Effect from current images, language, and robot state.

3

Decode and execute

Decode the predicted Effect into an action chunk, execute a prefix, and replan from new observations.
